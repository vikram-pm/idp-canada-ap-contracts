07 · AI approach

AI confidence is earned with evidence

1

Grounded

The model quotes the exact text it read. No quote, no value.

2

Consistent

A second, independent read must agree, compared by meaning.

3

Valid

Deterministic Canadian rules decide validity. The model only reads.

Not verified → a personNDA risk → always a personAI outage → Manual with retry

08 · Validation

Tested against requirements, then improved

8/8

acceptance scenarios passed on the test pack: 3 auto-approved, 5 to review for a real reason

What testing foundWhat changed

AI outages made documents vanishRoute to Manual with retry; block approval
