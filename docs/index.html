04 · AI approach

AI confidence is earned with evidence

Model self-reported confidence is poorly calibrated, so it isn't used. A field is verified only when all three checks pass.

1 · Grounded

The model returns the exact text it read. No quote, no value. Reviewers see it outlined on the PDF.

2 · Consistent

A second, independent read must agree, compared by meaning ("Quebec" = "QC", "4 874,94 $" = "$4,874.94").

3 · Valid

Deterministic Canadian rules decide validity. The model only reads; rules judge.

ConditionRoute
Every critical field verified, no NDA riskAuto-approve
A critical field missing, invalid or inconsistentReview field highlighted, Approve disabled
Non-Canadian governing law or Quebec party on an NDAReview always, Legal decides
AI unavailable or unreadable documentManual with Retry AI extraction

07 · Decisions & responsibility

Trade-offs I made on purpose

Product decisions

Dates as month/day/year (French invoices may differ; the printed value is always shown) · QST mandatory for Quebec · GST/HST number and duplicates as notes, accepting the input tax credit and duplicate-payment risk · Flash-Lite as default after Flash overloads.

Responsible AI

Source text on every value · approval blocked on open issues · reason codes and spot audit against automation bias · append-only audit trail · synthetic data only; PIPEDA and Quebec Law 25 assessment required before real data · status by icon and label, not colour alone.
